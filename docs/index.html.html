The Problem

Blood is perishable and life-critical, yet many blood banks still run on paper ledgers, with reception, the lab, and hospital dispatch working as separate silos.

No live visibilityStock by blood type, status, and expiry is not visible at a glance.
Preventable wastageUnits expire because older stock is not issued first.
Slow emergenciesFulfillment depends on manual search and phone calls.
Highest riskNothing reliably stops a donor with a positive viral test from donating again.

The Solution: one role-based workflow

  • Four live KPIs: available units, units under testing, critical shortages by blood type, open hospital requests.
  • All eight blood types tracked per unit, with status and expiry date.
  • The working MVP is built and demonstrated in a short walkthrough video.

Safety by Design

Medical decisions use fixed, auditable rules, never a generative or probabilistic model.

Release interlockNo unit becomes Available or is dispatched without a recorded clearance.
Auto-deferralA positive result fires a MySQL trigger: the donor becomes Medically Suspended and the reason is logged.
FEFO dispatchA stored procedure issues the valid unit closest to expiry first.
RBAC + auditEach role sees only what it needs, and critical actions are logged.

The rules live in the database, so they hold even if the application layer is bypassed.
